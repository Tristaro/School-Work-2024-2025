#### **1️⃣ Media as Technology**

- **How microtransactions are engineered into game design**
- **Psychological mechanics**: loot boxes, FOMO (fear of missing out), engagement loops
- **Persuasive design**: UI/UX nudging (e.g., easy one-click purchases, premium currency)
- **Relevant readings**:
    - **"The Content of Media Shapes Us"** → How digital interfaces shape user behavior
    - **"The Materiality of AI"** → AI-driven personalization in games
    - **"Computers, Networks, and Tracking"** → Data-driven decision-making in game monetization

---

#### **2️⃣ Media as Text**

- **The "pay-to-win" narrative** in gaming culture
- **Cultural divide**: free-to-play vs. premium models, player backlash
- **Ethical concerns**: fairness, accessibility, gambling mechanics
- **Relevant readings**:
    - **"Ads, Interpellation, and Ideological Codes"** → How in-game ads and microtransactions shape identities
    - **"Audiences, Negotiation, and Fandom"** → How different gaming communities interpret microtransactions

---

#### **3️⃣ Media as a Commodity**

- **Microtransactions as a dominant business model**
- **Loot boxes and gambling comparisons**
- **How game developers sustain revenue without selling new games**
- **Relevant readings**:
    - **"The Industrial Economy"** → Commodification of digital goods
    - **"Surveillance Economy"** → Monetizing player behavior through data
    - **"Efficiency and the McDonaldization of Society"** → Standardized monetization across games
