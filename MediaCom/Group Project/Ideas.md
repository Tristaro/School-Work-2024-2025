What topics (in relation to media studies) are you interested in exploring for this project? 

Microtransactions in Video Games (e.g., Fortnite, FIFA Ultimate Team, Gacha Games) 

Technology: How microtransactions are integrated into game design. 

Text: The narrative of “pay-to-win” and its impact on gaming culture. 

Commodity: The business model behind loot boxes and in-game purchases. 

The Algorithmic Influence of TikTok’s “For You” Page 

Technology: How AI curates content and impacts user engagement.  

Text: How TikTok videos are structured as media texts.  

Commodity: The role of TikTok in advertising, influencer marketing, and data monetization. 

The Psychology of YouTube Ads and Skippable Content 

Technology: How YouTube's ad system tracks user behavior. 

Text: The persuasive techniques used in 5-second ads. 

Commodity: How advertisers maximize engagement with non-skippable ads.
