## Individual Contribution Reflection

### My Contributions & Experience

For this project, I was responsible for analyzing **Media as Technology** and **Media as a Commodity**, as well as assisting in script writing, creating the presentation, and handling the **video editing**. Out of all these tasks, I enjoyed **video editing the most**, mainly because I didn’t find the research, writing, or coordination aspects very engaging.

If I could redo the project, I would focus on **completing my part as quickly as possible** and **avoiding tasks that required waiting on my group member**. Since I was in charge of editing, I had to wait for **recordings**, communicate about **revisions**, and discuss **changes**, which required more social interaction than I’m used to. This added extra stress to an already busy schedule, so in the future, I would prefer to work on sections that I can complete independently without needing as much back-and-forth discussion.

### Challenges & Reflections

My **workflow wasn’t ideal**, especially because this project was assigned at an inconvenient time when most of my focus was on **studying for midterms**. Additionally, reading week meant I was doing most of my work at home, which made scheduling and coordination more difficult. Despite these challenges, I managed to complete and submit everything on time, so I think the project turned out well overall.

One challenge I faced was a mix of both **a personal and a group issue**. When we were dividing tasks, my partner only wanted to do **one section** and assist with the presentation, leaving me with the majority of the work. Since I’m not the type of person to **argue or push back**, I just went along with it and ended up doing more. In hindsight, this didn’t feel like much of a challenge because I **preferred working on my own**—it allowed me to **maintain control over my research and writing**. We also disagreed on how to approach **Media as Text**, and his section overlapped with my topics (**Media as Technology** and **Media as a Commodity**). Rather than trying to fix it, I decided to **let him do his section his way** while I stuck to mine, which made things much easier.

With **three midterms** coming up at the same time as this project, I had **bigger priorities**, so I didn’t want to spend unnecessary time on disagreements. In the end, I’m **satisfied** with how everything turned out, and I’m just **glad it’s over** since I still have other assignments to complete.
