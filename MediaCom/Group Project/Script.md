## **Slide 2: Introduction to Microtransactions**

Microtransactions refer to small purchases that players make within video games. They can range from cosmetic items, like skins, to in-game advantages, such as power-ups. These purchases are prevalent in free-to-play mobile games, console titles, and gacha-based games. Over time, they have reshaped how players engage with games, making monetization an integral part of game design.
