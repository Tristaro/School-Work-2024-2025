### ✅ **Chapter 8: Producing Data – Sampling**

#### 🎯 **Goals**

- Understand **why sampling is needed** and how it supports inference.
    
- Learn different methods of **selecting samples**.
    
- Recognize **biases and errors** that can occur in sample surveys.
    

---

### 🧠 **Key Concepts**

#### 🔹 **Population vs. Sample**

- **Population**: The entire group we want information about.
    
- **Sample**: The part of the population from which we actually collect information.
    

#### 🔹 **Sampling Design**

- **Census**: Attempts to contact every individual in the population.
    
- **Sample survey**: Selects a sample to represent the population.
    

---

### 🔍 **Types of Sampling Methods**

|Method|Description|
|---|---|
|**Simple Random Sample (SRS)**|Every group of individuals has an equal chance of being selected.|
|**Stratified Random Sample**|Population is divided into strata (groups) and SRS is taken from each stratum.|
|**Cluster Sample**|Population is divided into clusters, and one or more clusters are randomly chosen to survey all individuals within.|
|**Multistage Sample**|Combines multiple sampling methods in stages.|

---

### 🚫 **Types of Bias**

|Type|Description|
|---|---|
|**Undercoverage**|Some groups are left out of the sampling process.|
|**Nonresponse**|Individuals selected do not respond.|
|**Response Bias**|Respondents lie or misremember (e.g., due to sensitive topics).|
|**Wording Effects**|The phrasing of questions affects responses.|

---

### 💻 **Modern Survey Methods**

- **Random Digit Dialing**: Often used in phone surveys, but affected by nonresponse and caller ID screening.
    
- **Internet Surveys**: Fast and low-cost but risk **voluntary response bias** and **undercoverage**.
