Normal Distribution ^I0i0dMPi

Bell-shaped, symmetric curve where most values cluster around the mean. ^CqUusZmr

Z-score ^VO63IfK9

Measures how many standard deviations a value is from the mean. ^h8aBBe87

Formula: z = (x - μ) / σ ^YZPUbnF1
