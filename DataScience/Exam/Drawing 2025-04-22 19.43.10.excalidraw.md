Normal Distribution ^I0i0dMPi

Bell-shaped, symmetric curve where most values cluster around the mean. ^CqUusZmr

Example: IQ scores follow a normal distribution with mean 100 and standard deviation 15. ^t0Tqi4ga

Z-score ^VO63IfK9

Measures how many standard deviations a value is from the mean. ^h8aBBe87

Formula: z = (x - μ) / σ ^YZPUbnF1

Example: If SAT scores have a mean of 500 and standard deviation of 100, a score of 650 has a z-score of (650-500)/100 = 1.5. ^eJrANH2q

Residuals ^k1XfHFGE

Difference between actual and predicted values in regression. ^KXULVHl7

Example: If actual weight is 80 kg and predicted is 78 kg, residual is 80 - 78 = 2. ^rYN5uE7u
